## Intent
First film of the v3 lesson series for Candle Quest Academy. One continuous chart of a
full futures day (6 PM → 4 PM Eastern) is drawn left to right while the narrator names
each session: Asia (quiet box), London (first real move, pokes past Asia), New York
(8:30 news, 9:30 bell, fastest), lunch lull, power hour (3–4 PM). Ends on the question
"What time is it?".
